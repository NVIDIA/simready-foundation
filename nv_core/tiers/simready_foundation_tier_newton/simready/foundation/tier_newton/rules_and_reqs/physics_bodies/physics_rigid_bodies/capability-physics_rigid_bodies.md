# Physics Rigid Bodies

**Capability:** Physics Rigid Bodies (RB)

## Overview

This capability covers rigid-body and collider authoring for physics simulation.

The Newton tier contributes the Newton-solver rigid-body requirements for this
capability (Newton collider schema, Newton SDF attributes, Newton mass
attributes); the base rigid-body and collider requirements are owned by the Core
tier.
