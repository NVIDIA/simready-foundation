# Physics Driven Joints

**Capability:** Physics Driven Joints (DJ)

## Overview

This capability covers driven-joint and articulation-actuation authoring for
physics simulation.

The Newton tier contributes the Newton-solver driven-joint requirements for this
capability (Newton joint drive/actuator, Newton joint attributes, Newton mimic
coupling); the base driven-joint requirements are owned by the Core tier.
