## Description

Defines the PhysX driven-joint contract, adding PhysX-compatible drive, mimic, velocity, and drive-value requirements to robot joint authoring.

## Authoring and Calibration Notes

- **Joint-state and drive schema placement (`DJ.001`, `DJ.002`, `DJ.003`, `RC.005`, `RC.006`):** Apply axis-specific `PhysicsJointStateAPI:angular` (revolute) or `PhysicsJointStateAPI:linear` (prismatic) and `PhysicsDriveAPI:<axis>` in `payloads/Physics/physics.usda` (not `payloads/robot.usda` or `base.usda`), with finite `drive:<axis>:physics:maxForce > 0` and matching `drive:<axis>:physics:targetPosition` / `state:<axis>:physics:position` consistent with the composed link rest pose (`DJ.003`). Preserve valid winding and rest coordinates when `targetPosition`, `state:<axis>:physics:position`, and link transforms are consistent and within `[lowerLimit, upperLimit]` (do not replace a valid `-360 deg` state/target on a finite-limited `[-360, 360] deg` joint with `0 deg` while leaving limits unchanged merely to fit `state_accuracy` waypoint pacing, as that alters legal travel relative to the mechanical stops; if the coordinate convention itself requires conversion from source evidence, transform limits, states, targets, and couplings consistently together). On joints whose `[lowerLimit, upperLimit]` excludes `0 deg`, keep `targetPosition`, `state:<axis>:physics:position`, and the child link transforms at an in-range rest pose so `effort_limit` and `DJ.003` pass.
- **Proportional drive damping when capping stiffness (`DJ.006`) and preserving source joint friction:** `DJ.006` enforces `0.0 <= drive:<axis>:physics:stiffness <= 1e6`. When capping extreme source stiffness (`1e7..1e15`) to `<= 1e6` in `runnables/physics/physx.usda`, reduce `drive:<axis>:physics:damping` proportionally (keeping $\tau = k_d / k_p \in [0.003, 0.2]\,\text{s}$) so an unscaled `1e11..1e14` damping value does not freeze the arm in `full_range_sweep`, `state_accuracy`, and `multi_joint_coordination`. Preserve source-authored `physxJoint:jointFriction` (require concrete evidence of a mistaken source conversion before altering friction rather than removing friction because it opposes PD tracking; otherwise diagnose controller authority, test pacing, or a runtime limitation).
- **Mimic followers and closed-loop 4-bar linkages (`DJ.004`, `DJ.006`, `DJ.007`, `DJ.011`):**
  - Revolute `PhysxMimicJointAPI` followers must set `drive:angular:physics:stiffness = 0` and `damping = 0`.
  - Prismatic `PhysxMimicJointAPI` followers require positive `drive:linear:physics:stiffness > 0` under `DJ.004`/`DJ.006`; use a minimal positive follower stiffness (`drive:linear:physics:stiffness = 1`, `damping = 0`) in `runnables/physics/physx.usda` when a large follower spring (`1000`) holding `targetPosition = 0` would oppose `PhysxMimicJointAPI` during `mimic_joint`.
  - `DJ.007` requires finite non-zero `physxMimicJoint:<axis>:naturalFrequency` (use `-1` for a rigid hard-constraint sentinel or a positive compliant frequency such as `20`).
  - On non-excluded passive joints in a closed-loop 4-bar linkage (`DJ.011`) that do not carry `PhysxMimicJointAPI`, satisfy `DJ.004` by applying `PhysicsDriveAPI:angular` with `stiffness = 0`, `damping = 0`, and a positive `maxForce > 0` so no active spring fights the primary loop driver.
