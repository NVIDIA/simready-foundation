# Requirements

## Summary

- **AC-powered equipment must declare its nominal input voltage.**
- **AC-powered equipment must declare its power rating.**
- **AC-powered equipment must declare its input frequency.**
- **AC-powered equipment must have `electrical_nominal_voltage` connection point prims.**
