# Physics Materials

**Capability:** Physics Materials (PMT)

## Overview

This capability enables the definition and application of physical material
properties to surfaces and objects in physics simulations.

The Newton tier contributes the Newton-solver material requirement for this
capability (`NewtonMaterialAPI` placement); the base physics-material
requirements are owned by the Core tier.
